ABOUT US

FIND YOUR PLACE AT NEWLIFE FOUNTAIN MINISTRIES

At Newlife Fountain Ministries, we are a Christ-centered church dedicated to sharing the Gospel and demonstrating God's love through compassion, service, and community outreach. Through education, healthcare, charitable initiatives, and support for vulnerable families, we strive to bring hope and transform lives.
Whether you're seeking faith, healing, purpose, or a place to belong, you are welcome here. Together, we are making a lasting difference
one life, one family, and one relationship at a time. You are loved. You are valued. You are welcome. Together, let's transform lives through faith, compassion, and service.

All The Charity We Provide

A Different Kind of Community

Join our monthly giving program to provide consistent support to our initiatives. Regular contributions, no matter the size, help us plan and sustain long-term projects.

Quality Education

Empowering children and communities through access to quality education, learning resources, mentorship, and opportunities that unlock lifelong potential.

Nutritious Food

Fighting hunger by delivering nutritious meals and food assistance to families and communities facing food insecurity.

Healthcare Services

Providing accessible and quality healthcare services to underserved communities, ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to receive the medical care they need.

Clean Water

Ensuring access to safe and clean water for all communities, particularly those in need, by implementing sustainable water solutions and infrastructure.

Women Empowerment

Supporting and empowering women in our community through education, mentorship, and leadership development opportunities.

Skills Development

Offering vocational training and practical skills that empower individuals to secure employment, start businesses, and build brighter futures.
